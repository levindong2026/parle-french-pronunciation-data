# French nasal vowels: spot the word and use it

**A1 classroom activity · 10–12 minutes · English and French instructions**

Learners match a heard word to its meaning, explain a spelling clue and use the word in a short reply. You need this sheet and the [three free vowel reference clips](https://getparle.app/blog/french-an-en-in-on-pronunciation/#article-audio-title). They are synthesized examples of the vowels by themselves.

## Prepare the three choices

| A | B | C |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **un banc** — a bench | **bon** — good | **un bain** — a bath |
| Target /ɑ̃/ | Target /ɔ̃/ | Target /ɛ̃/ |

Play each reference once. Point to the corresponding column. Then read **banc**, **bon** and **bain** at a comfortable pace so that learners can connect each isolated sound to a full word. These symbols represent three practice categories. Pronunciation varies by speaker and region.

Écoutez une fois chaque voyelle de référence. Associez-la à une colonne. Écoutez ensuite les trois mots entiers, lus à un rythme naturel.

## Student sheet

### 1. Listen and pick a column · Écouter et choisir

Write **A**, **B** or **C** for each word you hear. Your partner keeps the word order out of sight.
Notez **A**, **B** ou **C** pour chaque mot entendu. Votre partenaire garde l'ordre des mots caché.

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ____ | ____ | ____ | ____ | ____ | ____ |

Working alone? Play the labelled reference clips, say each whole word, then start at task 2. The clips model the isolated vowels; use a partner for the hidden-word round.

### 2. Read a new scene and say the full reply · Répondre en français

1. You're tired at the park. Say **On peut s’asseoir sur le …**
2. You taste the soup and like it. Say **C’est … !**
3. A child is getting ready for bed. Ask **Tu veux prendre un … ?**

Say all three without looking at the table. Then name a spelling clue for each word: **an**, **on** or **ain**.
Donnez les trois réponses sans regarder le tableau. Ensuite, repérez **an**, **on** ou **ain** dans chaque mot.

### 3. Change the speaker · Changer de rôle

Swap partners or roles. One person chooses the park, soup or bedtime situation and describes it in any language. The other gives the French reply from memory. If the listener hears a different word, repeat the whole reply at a comfortable pace.

Changez de rôle. Une personne décrit une des trois situations. L’autre formule la réplique en français de mémoire. Si le mot entendu ne correspond pas à la situation, reprenez la phrase entière à un rythme naturel.

<details>
<summary>Teacher script and explained answers · Script et corrigé</summary>

**Task 1.** Read these words, with about three seconds between them: **bain, banc, bon, bain, bon, banc**. The key is **C, A, B, C, B, A**. After checking, read any two missed words again in a different order and have learners give their meanings.

**Task 2.**

1. **On peut s’asseoir sur le banc.** In the park, **un banc** is a place to sit; **an** is the vowel spelling.
2. **C’est bon !** **Bon** describes how the soup tastes; **on** is the vowel spelling.
3. **Tu veux prendre un bain ?** **Prendre un bain** means to take a bath; **ain** is the vowel spelling.

If someone confuses **bain** and **banc**, use the meanings as prompts and listen to the two vowel references once before repeating. If **bon** gets lost among the other two, compare it with the rounded reference in the chart. This is a brief classroom check of word recognition and recall.
